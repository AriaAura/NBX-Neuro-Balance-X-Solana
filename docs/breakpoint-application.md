**Project name**
NBX (NeuroBalanceX)

**One-liner**
A dopamine-recovery app for the Solana Seeker where every clean day is recorded on-chain as Proof of Streak and funds a real meal.

**What are you building on Solana?**
NBX helps people quit compulsive habits (porn, weed, doomscrolling) and rebuild focus, using urge-surfing and dopamine-reset programs. That gives users a recovery record they own and can verify, and it is the ledger behind our Food Pool: one verified day unlocks one meal funded from NBX revenue. Seeker owners are our launch audience and we're building for the Solana dApp Store first.
